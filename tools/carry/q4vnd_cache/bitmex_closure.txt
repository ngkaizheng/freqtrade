 [Notice] BitMEX Exchange Now Closed

 Published 23 September 2026 

 Today BitMEX has ceased all exchange operations, as
 announced on 23 July 2026
 and has communicated to users by email regularly since the
 announcement. Trading, deposits, and new positions are no longer
 available on the platform.

 Your funds remain safe. Login and withdrawal
 capabilities remain available, and we strongly encourage all users to
 withdraw their remaining withdrawable balances as soon as possible.
 Please check your account e-mail inbox for withdrawal instructions and
 upcoming operational changes.

 Thank you to everyone who has been part of the BitMEX community. It
 has been an honour building the platform that defined crypto
 derivatives trading.

 here .

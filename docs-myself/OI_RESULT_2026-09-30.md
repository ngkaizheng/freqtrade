## 2. The measurement instrument was validated BEFORE the verdict, and it was broken twice

`check_ic_t.py` runs the exact function Gate 2's verdict depends on, on synthetic data where
the right answer is known.

| version | the bug | what it would have printed |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | `x = x - x.mean()` then returned `x.mean()/se` — **the mean after centring is 0** | **every t = 0.00 → all features "failed" → "OI carries no information"** |
| v2 | the raw product of the two rank series as the test statistic; under the null `E[rank_x·rank_y] ≈ ((n+1)/2)² ≈ 1.6e7` | **t = +101 on pure noise, firing 100 %** |

Corrected: standardise each rank series, take the Fisher transform of the product, Newey-West
on that. Final self-test — **NULL fires 4.3 % (want ~5 %) · NW/naive = 0.411 · power 74 % at
IC≈0.04 · NW cuts firing from 49 % to 10.7 %.**

> **One bug wearing a false negative, plus one that made noise look significant. This is the
> same shape this project has hit for 40 rounds, and the only reason it was caught BEFORE the
> conclusion rather than after is that the instrument had a control.**

**A third fault was found on real data by `check_oi_pipeline.py`**: 97 of ~450,000
`sum_open_interest` values are exactly **zero** (none are NaN). Open interest cannot be zero on
a live perp, so these are glitches — and a zero in `pct_change(k)` poisons **k bars after it**,
not just its own. They are now neutralised *before* differencing, and the count is printed.
**That same check asserts LOOK-AHEAD: 0 violations, median OI lag 0.33 h behind its 4h bar.**

## 5. Verdict, and how the two gates are weighed

**The preregistered decision tree returns `INCONCLUSIVE`** — Gate 2 fails on the IC leg
(best 0.0059 against a 0.02 bar) and short-circuits. That is reported as the verdict.

**Gate 3 is nevertheless the binding evidence, and the reason is not a new rule.** The
preregistration's own Gate 3 criterion reads:

> 「只有当 OI 特征的 IC **显著优于对照组**，才算 OI 贡献了新信息。」

Gate 3 is a **comparative** test — two t-statistics on the same data — and so does **not**
need the ~0.08 absolute threshold that Appendix A.3 attached to Gate 2. It fails decisively
in both reads, and by a margin that grows with sample size.

**So: `CLOSED ON GATE 3` (comparative), with `INCONCLUSIVE` recorded on Gate 2.**
Stated plainly because the tree said INCONCLUSIVE and the closure is a judgement.

## 6. What was actually learned

**(a) OI carries a real, consistent, statistically significant effect — and the sign matches
the literature.** Rising open interest predicts LOWER forward returns, same sign across all
three lookbacks and all three OI horizons, strongest at t = −4.99. New positions arriving is
crowding, and crowding precedes underperformance. **This is a genuine mechanism, not noise.**

**(b) It is not a third information source, which is what this project needed.** Its best
|t| is 4.22 against 8.85 for plain 4h price momentum and 7.65 for volume change. **OI is a
weaker echo of information the two existing books already carry.**

**(c) ⚠ AND THE CONTROLS POINT AT THE NEXT HYPOTHESIS.** `dVol_4` — the change in kline
volume — is the **second strongest predictor measured anywhere in this project** (t = +7.65,
IC = +0.0188). It is **not in the closed registry**: entry #11 closed *taker* flow because the
L1/L2 data was absent, which is a different data type from kline volume. **Tier 2 item F
( Kline-volume flow) was on the list; the control table has now promoted it from "worth trying"
to "the strongest measured candidate we have not tested".**

**(d) `mom_4` being strong is not news** — it independently confirms the delivered short book,
which is a trend book. What §17 closed was **cross-sectional** momentum at 1d/3d/1w.
**Single-asset time-series momentum was never in the registry** and remains Tier 2 item C.

## 7. What this does not say

* **Not "open interest is useless."** The effect is real and significant; it is simply weaker
  than what is already held, and ~0.006 IC is below the 0.02 bar.
* **Not a claim about the whole OI literature.** 4h bars, this universe, 2023-2026, one
  feature set. Intraday OI dynamics are a different question and are not addressed.
* **No strategy was built**, so nothing here is a backtest result. Gate 2/3 is a **feature
  layer** test and is not reported as a strategy result.
